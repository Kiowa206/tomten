# TomTen — iPhone Installation Guide

## What you need
- A computer with internet access (one time only)
- A free account at GitHub (github.com) — takes 2 minutes to create
- Your iPhone 17 connected to the same Wi-Fi as your computer (or any Wi-Fi, just once)

---

## Step 1 — Upload the game files

1. Go to **github.com** and sign in (or create a free account).
2. Click the **+** button (top right) → **New repository**.
3. Name it `tomten`, tick **"Add a README file"**, click **Create repository**.
4. Inside the new repo, click **Add file → Upload files**.
5. Drag all four files from this folder into the upload box:
   - `index.html`
   - `sw.js`
   - `manifest.json`
   - `icon-192.svg`
   - `icon-512.svg`
6. Click **Commit changes**.

---

## Step 2 — Enable GitHub Pages (free hosting)

1. In your repo, click **Settings** (top menu).
2. Scroll down to **Pages** (left sidebar).
3. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Set Branch to **main**, folder to **/ (root)**, click **Save**.
5. Wait about 60 seconds, then refresh — you'll see a green banner with your URL:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/tomten/`

---

## Step 3 — Install on your iPhone

1. On your iPhone, open **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome).
2. Go to the URL from Step 2: `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/tomten/`
3. Tap the **Share** button (the box with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
5. Tap **Add** in the top right.

TomTen now appears on your home screen like any other app.

---

## Step 4 — Play offline

Once installed, **TomTen works with no Wi-Fi or cellular connection**.
The game is fully stored on your phone after the first visit.

---

## That's it!

The game icon on your home screen opens TomTen full-screen with no browser bar,
exactly like a native app.
